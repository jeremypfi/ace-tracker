▸ What is ACE?

Accumulated Cyclone Energy measures the combined strength and duration of tropical storms and hurricanes. It's calculated by squaring a storm's maximum sustained wind speed (in knots) every six hours while it's at tropical storm strength or greater, then dividing by 10,000. A long-lived major hurricane generates far more ACE than a brief tropical storm, which makes ACE a better measure of overall season activity than storm counts alone.
